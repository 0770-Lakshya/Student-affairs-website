02 · Guidance for Freshers

Student Mentorship Program

Our student mentorship program helps freshers transition smoothly into college life. Experienced seniors guide you academically, personally, and socially, ensuring you make informed decisions throughout your journey at IIT Bhilai.

About SMP

The Student Mentorship Program is a student-run initiative to help freshers navigate campus life, academics, and opportunities at IIT Bhilai.

Our Objective

A lot of things runs into a Freshie's mind during his initial stay at the institute. We are here to clear those clouds of apprehensiveness. Here, we aim to provide info to all the freshers and give them insight to the life @iitbhilai. If you are more intrigued, then you can simply contact us and we would have your question answered in the best possible way.

Who Are The Mentors?

They are a group of seniors who will be helping the juniors in exploring their options at the same time in progressing forward. This site is an online implementation of the mentorship scheme. An offline version of this will involve a senior being assigned as a mentor to a group of freshers to guide them in making informed decisions.

Message from the Director

Prof. Rajiv Prakash Director, IIT Bhilai

"It gives me immense pleasure to congratulate you on your admission to the Indian Institute of Technology Bhilai and to extend a warm welcome to our academic family. Since our establishment in 2016 and shifting to our permanent campus in 2023, the Institute has expanded its academic offerings considerably. Today, we proudly offer B.Tech., M.Tech., M.Sc., and Ph.D. programs across various disciplines."

The years ahead will be among the most enriching, transformative, and rewarding phases of your life. To ensure that every student receives the guidance needed during this transition, we have established the Student Mentorship Program (SMP). Designed to facilitate your smooth progression from JEE achievers to confident IITians, our dedicated student mentors are committed to helping you settle into campus life.

The IITs stand at the forefront of scientific discovery, innovation, and technological advancement. As members of IIT Bhilai, I encourage you to embrace this legacy and dedicate yourselves to advancing the aspirations of our nation. Striving for excellence, we will successfully meet any challenges and create a lasting impact. I wish you every success as you embark on this exciting new chapter."

Warm regards,
Prof. Rajiv Prakash
· Message from SMP Faculty In-Charge

Message from the FIC, SMP

Dr. Praveen Kumar FIC, Student Mentorship Program

On behalf of the team of the Student Mentorship Program, I welcome newly admitted students to the new and vibrant campus of IIT Bhilai. The Student Mentorship Program at IIT Bhilai organises orientation programs for the new batch of students to get familiar with academics, institute facilities, student life, and various events. The institute provides a friendly environment and ample opportunities to excel in technical skills and actively participate in co-curricular activities.

The team of the Student Mentorship Program is dedicated to providing all new students with the assistance and support they need.

Once again, many congratulations to all new students on being admitted to IIT Bhilai. Best wishes for a memorable stay at IIT Bhilai and a successful career.

Best wishes,
Dr. Praveen Kumar
