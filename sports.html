Healthy mind, healthy body

Sports

Grounds, courts and coaching for every level, from casual evening games to the teams that represent IIT Bhilai at the Inter-IIT Sports Meet.

Cricket

A maintained pitch, practice nets and floodlights for evening sessions.

Football

Open field and a squad that plays at the Inter-IIT Sports Meet every year.

Basketball

Outdoor courts and a club that welcomes newcomers, whatever their level.

Volleyball

Men's and women's teams with strong Inter-IIT showings.

Badminton

Two indoor courts in every hostel, with rackets available.

Table tennis

Fast, focused and strategic, with tables in the hostels.

Athletics

Track and field training on the main ground, the venue for Inter-IIT preparation and Prayatna.

Chess

Regular tournaments and casual games for players of every rating.

Yoga

Sessions to build strength and flexibility, and to unwind from academic stress.

Recreational sports

Kabaddi, kho kho, dodgeball, frisbee, carrom and more, just for fun.

Main ground (cricket, football and athletics): behind Shivnath Hostel (MSH). Badminton and table tennis: inside every hostel.

Prayatna, the flagship inter-batch sports tournament, runs for two months every year. Selections for the Inter-IIT Sports Meet happen through the Sports Council.
