From the DoSA office

Read the Hostel Manual and follow the SOPs

The Hostel Manual (January 2026) sets out hostel and mess rules and the Standard Operating Procedures for complaints, grievances and emergencies. Raise routine matters at your hostel first, and avoid mass mailing. Genuine grievances will always be heard, and emergencies should be reported immediately.

Hostel Manual (PDF)

Raising a hostel complaint

For maintenance, housekeeping, Wi-Fi, plumbing, electrical, furniture, water supply and other routine issues.

  1. Report it at your hostelUse the hostel complaint register, visit the hostel office, or email your hostel office (below).
  2. If not fixed within a week?Escalate through the channel in the Hostel Manual SOP, starting with your hostel's warden.
  3. Emergency? Don't waitFor fire, medical, electrical, security, violence or ragging, go straight to hostel staff, security, your warden or the DoSA office if needed.

Hostel rules at a glance

A summary only. The Hostel Manual is the final word.

Grievances

Grievances about hostel administration, allocation, discipline, mess administration, staff behaviour, harassment, discrimination or hostel policy can be raised through the grievance SOP. Every grievance is kept confidential, no student will face retaliation for a genuine complaint, and anonymous complaints may be considered if backed by sufficient evidence.

Timings and visitors

Hostel residents may not stay out of campus between 11 pm and 6 am. Visitors, including parents, sign the visitors' book at the gate and may use only common areas between 7 am and 9 pm. Students visiting a hostel of the opposite gender are allowed only in common areas, until 10 pm. Hostel sports areas are open until 10 pm.

Leaving the campus

Submit Form HO-02 to your hostel office at least one day before you leave. MTech and PhD students attach their approved academic leave. Mark exit and entry in the hostel register and at the biometric gates, and always carry your ID card.

In your room

Rooms are allotted by the hostel office, and room changes need the warden's permission. Personal kettles, heaters, irons, induction plates, fans, fridges and ACs are not allowed. Get your room cleaned by housekeeping at least twice a week, and never go onto the rooftop or terrace.

Zero tolerance

Smoking, alcohol, drugs and ragging are strictly prohibited, with fines and referral to the disciplinary committee. Report any such activity to hostel staff or your warden. Your identity will be kept confidential.

Health and wellbeing

Report any illness or accident to the hostel office and health centre straight away (Health Centre 07882991612 or 09424283691, ambulance 07647068419). For stress, anxiety or low mood, the Institute Counselling Services and Health Centre are there to help, in confidence.

Penalties and appeals

Violations can bring fines, set out in the manual, and repeated or serious ones go to the disciplinary committee. Appeals against a penalty must normally be made within seven working days of the order.
