You are not alone

Student Well-being

Feeling low, stressed or just need someone to talk to? Help is always close by — here is where to turn.

Dr. Eslavath Rajkumar Chairperson, Happiness and Wellness Centre

SETU Wellness Club

The student-run wellness club. It led a month-long World Suicide Prevention Day campaign and ran Gatekeeper Training for the security team (21–22 August 2026), so that help is always nearby.

Samvad

Open dialogue sessions where students, faculty and staff sit together and talk. Most recently, the Khelo India Samvad brought the campus together on National Sports Day 2026.

Happiness and Wellness Centre

Chaired by Dr. Eslavath Rajkumar, the Centre anchors student well-being on campus — the first stop for wellness support and a listening ear.

Health Centre

Sushrut Bhawan is a 24x7 health centre with an ambulance, a short walk from the hostels.

+91 94242 83691 healthcenter@iitbhilai.ac.in

Need to talk to someone right now?

Tele-MANAS is the Government of India's free, 24x7 mental-health helpline. You can also reach the DoSA office directly — your identity will be protected.
