Hostel

Everything about living on campus, in one place. Open a topic to read more.

Room allotment

Rooms are allotted to new and continuing students at the start of each semester. Submit the allotment form from the Forms page before the deadline announced by the office.

Mess and dining

Hostel messes serve breakfast, lunch, snacks and dinner. Menu changes and feedback go through the mess committee.

Maintenance requests

Report electrical, plumbing or furniture issues to your hostel caretaker or through the warden's office. Include your room number and a short description.

Rules and visitors

Hostel rules cover entry timings, guests and shared spaces. Please read them at check-in and follow them to keep the hostel safe for everyone.

Leaving and returning

Apply for leave in advance using the leave form, and complete the no-dues process before vacating your room at the end of your stay.
