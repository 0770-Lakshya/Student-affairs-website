Council of Student Affairs

CoSA is the elected student council. It runs the cultural, sci-tech, sports and academic wings, and oversees every club and association on campus.

Major council events

The events that mark the student year.

Prayatna

The flagship two-month inter-batch sports tournament, from chess and table tennis to football and athletics.

Ciao

The freshers' cultural welcome, where every club shows what it does and new students find their people.

Anveshan

A week-long intra-institute cultural championship across music, dance, drama, fine arts, quizzing and more.

Cultscape

The closing celebration of the cultural year, a stage for everyone's talent before the session ends.

CoSA also brings the campus together for Diwali, Holi, Ganesh Chaturthi, Navratri, Janmashtami, Makar Sankranti kite flying, Gudi Padwa, Independence Day, Republic Day and New Year.

Cultural and technical festival

Meraz

The annual techno-cultural fest of IIT Bhilai.

Celebrating Innovation, Art and Creativity

Meraz - the pulsating heart of IIT Bhilai — is where technology, art, and imagination unite to create an unforgettable celebration of innovation and culture. Evolving with each edition, Meraz has grown into a grand stage for talent, collaboration, and creative exploration. From cutting-edge tech showcases and coding battles to captivating performances, fashion artistry, and culinary flair, Meraz embraces every shade of human creativity.
